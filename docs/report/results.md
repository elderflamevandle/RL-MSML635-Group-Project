## Limitations

- **Single seed per cell.** Compute budget doesn't permit multi-seed
  variance estimates. The plan's Week-6 buffer may add a replication seed
  for the winner.
- **LJ is a prompted LLM, not a frozen rubric.** Variance across Groq
  routing could affect scores; mitigated by `temperature = 0` and on-disk
  caching keyed by prompt+response hash.
- **No multi-GPU scaling.** All runs single-GPU + LoRA. Full fine-tune
  numbers are out of scope.
- **Qwen3 training-corpus overlap with HotpotQA** is not independently
  audited. If present, our absolute numbers overestimate real-world
  performance; relative reward comparisons remain valid.
